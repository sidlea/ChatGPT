# Social content ideas built for 2026 algorithms

> Part of the Hallgate playbook for Cassiel Bar & Kitchen and Raph's Lounge, Cottingham. Prices, dates and margins are working assumptions (Oct 2026): check them against real recipe costs, licences and calendars before use.

## 4. Social campaign ideas (24), built for the 2026 algorithms

### 4.0 How to run social with two time-poor owners
- **Platform roles:**
  - **Instagram Reels**: the main discovery and "send to a friend" channel [S9].
  - **TikTok**: "Nearby" local discovery [S8]. Post natively from the venue, with Cottingham and Hull in the captions.
  - **Facebook**: Events (live music, quiz, NYE), local community groups, and the 35–65 audience.
  - **Google Business Profile**: weekly Offer and Event posts, the menu, and photos (it converts searchers who are ready to book).
  - **WhatsApp Channel "Hallgate Insider"**: last-minute tables and first access to offers.
  - **Email**: fortnightly in Oct–Nov, weekly in December.
  - **SMS**: only for deadlines (3–4 a quarter).
- **Content pillars and mix:**
  - **Proof** (food and drink close-ups, the kitchen process) 40%
  - **People** (chefs, bar team, owners, locals) 25%
  - **Plans** (offers, events, booking calls to action) 25%
  - **Place** (Hallgate, the old bank, Cottingham) 10%
- **Weekly cadence for each venue:**
  - Cassiel: 4 Reels or TikToks, 1 carousel, Stories every trading day, 2 GBP posts, 1–2 WhatsApp Channel posts
  - Raph's: 3 Reels, Stories on gig and quiz nights, 2 GBP posts (Events), 1–2 WhatsApp posts
  - 1 joint "Hallgate" Collab post a week (posted to both accounts through Instagram's Collab feature)
- **The Tuesday Content Hour (2–3pm, the quiet slot):** one person films 8–10 clips across both venues from a shot list taken from the Content Brain. Staff film 15-second clips on gig nights. n8n schedules everything else. Kit: a phone, a £30 clip-mic and a £15 light. That's all.
- **Hooks are the whole game.** Show the most appetising or surprising frame in the first second, put on-screen text that names Cottingham or Hull, keep clips 15–35s, use captions with a single call to action, and include **"send this to…"** prompts (sends are a top-ranked signal [S9]). Test new hooks as **Trial Reels** to non-followers first [S9].
- **Disclosure:** any gifted creator content must be labelled **#ad** or **#gifted** (ASA/CMA rules on influencer marketing). Competitions need short T&Cs (CAP Code section 8).

### 4.1 The ideas

**1. "We heard you": the owner's honest video (Raph's)**
- **Platforms:** Instagram Reel, FB (native upload), TikTok. Pin it on all three.
- **Hook (0–3s):** Simon on the pavement between the two venues: *"Some of you told us the food at Raph's wasn't good enough. You were right."*
- **Script outline:**
  1. Who we are: "Me and my brother Chris run Raph's and Cassiel, right here on Hallgate."
  2. "We read every review. A couple this month stung, because they were fair."
  3. B-roll of the changes, with "Real mozzarella. Dough made here. Chicken marinated overnight, grilled to order. A shorter menu we can nail every time."
  4. The ask: "Before we shout about it, taste it. Two tasting nights, 30 seats each. Comment TASTE."
  5. Sign-off: "Be honest with us. That's the deal."
- **Cadence:** a one-off on Tue 27 Oct, then V2 on 13 Nov and V3 on 13 Jan (section 6).
- **Expected outcome:** the highest-reach post of the quarter in local feeds (honest owner content gets shared widely in village groups); 100+ TASTE comments; a reputational reset. **The video only works if the changes are real.**

**2. "Kitchen Reset": a 4-part mini-docuseries (Raph's)**
- **Format:** Reels and TikTok, 30–45s each.
- **Hook:** ep. 1, *"Real mozzarella, dough made here. Watch."*
- **Episodes:**
  1. The real-mozzarella stretch test
  2. 48-hour dough, from ball to blister
  3. The new chicken: marinade, grill, slice
  4. Tasting-night reactions, unscripted and including one honest criticism and how you fixed it
- **Cadence:** weekly, Thursdays, 5 Nov to 26 Nov.
- **Expected outcome:** saves and shares from foodies; credibility for the reveal; footage that can be reused in ads.

**3. "Meet the Chef / Meet the Bar" (both)**
- **Format:** a 25s Reel, with each member of staff answering three quick questions.
- **Hook:** *"I've cooked for [X] years and this is the one dish I'd eat on my last day."*
- **Script:** name and where they're from → the dish they're proudest of, cooked on camera → "Come and say hi on [day]".
- **Cadence:** one a fortnight, rotating through about 10 kitchen staff and the bar team.
- **Expected outcome:** puts human faces on both venues (important for Raph's trust); helps staff retention; strong Facebook engagement.

**4. The "£25 on Hallgate" challenge (both)**
- **Format:** Reel or TikTok in a first-person challenge style.
- **Hook:** *"How far does £25 go on Hallgate?"* with a running price counter on screen.
- **Script:** lunch tapas 2 for £11.90 at Cassiel → cross the road → a pint at Raph's → total on screen → "Change left over."
- **Variants:** a £15 lunch, a £40 date night, "Christmas party for under £40".
- **Cadence:** monthly.
- **Expected outcome:** value perception without discounting [S1]; strong sends ("we should do this").

**5. "Behind the Bar in 30 Seconds" (Cassiel)**
- **Format:** an overhead Reel of a cocktail build, with a voice-over or text recipe.
- **Hook:** *"Hull Fair in a glass."* (Brandy Snap Espresso Martini), then seasonal versions: Toffee Apple Toddy, Mince Pie Old Fashioned, a 0% Spritz in January.
- **Script:** ingredients on screen → build → pour shot → "Ours is £X, or make it at home."
- **Cadence:** weekly.
- **Expected outcome:** saves (a strong signal); positions Cassiel as *the* cocktail bar.

**6. Advent Cocktail Calendar (Cassiel, 1–24 Dec)**
- **Format:** a daily Story "door", plus 3 Reels a week.
- **Hook:** *"Door 7: you'll want to send this one to your sister."*
- **Script:** a door opens → the drink is revealed → "Today only, £X", or "comment the emoji to win one".
- **Cadence:** daily Stories; Reels on Mon, Wed and Fri.
- **Expected outcome:** a daily reason to visit the profile; Story replies count as DMs (a strong signal); drives same-day footfall.

**7. "Staff Picks Friday" (both)**
- **Format:** quick-cut Reel.
- **Hook:** *"We gave our team £30 to spend here. This is what they ordered."*
- **Cadence:** fortnightly.
- **Expected outcome:** honest recommendations; nudges spend toward high-GP items.

**8. "Live at Raph's" (Raph's)**
- **Format:** a 15–20s band clip filmed by staff on the night, posted the next morning, plus a FB Event for the next gig.
- **Hook:** loudest chorus moment plus the text *"This was Saturday on Hallgate."*
- **Script:** clip → "Next Saturday: [band]. Free entry. Book a table." Tag the band so they share it.
- **Cadence:** after every gig.
- **Expected outcome:** bands share to their fanbases (free reach); builds the "Hallgate is where the night is" identity.

**9. "Friday Night Countdown" (both)**
- **Format:** Stories and the WhatsApp Channel, Fri 4pm.
- **Hook:** *"3 tables left tonight. First to message gets them."*
- **Cadence:** every Friday, and also on Saturdays in December.
- **Expected outcome:** fills late gaps; trains followers to check the channel; real scarcity (never fake it).

**10. "Hallgate Faces": a locals spotlight (both)**
- **Format:** a carousel or 30s Reel, posted to IG and to Cottingham FB groups where the group rules allow.
- **Hook:** *"Brenda's been coming in every Thursday since we opened."*
- **Script:** a regular, a neighbouring independent, or a local club, with a photo and three questions about Cottingham → tag them.
- **Cadence:** weekly.
- **Expected outcome:** community goodwill; tagged people share; cross-promotion with other Hallgate and King Street independents.

**11. "Castle Hill Crew": NHS appreciation (both)**
- **Format:** FB, IG, GBP post, and a Blue Light Card listing [S20].
- **Hook:** *"Just finished a 12-hour shift at Castle Hill? This one's for you."*
- **Script:** an offer card (section 5, offer #6) → "Tag a colleague who deserves this" → monthly **"Nominate a Hero"**: a meal for 4 for one nominated team.
- **Cadence:** launch W4, then weekly reminders and a monthly winner.
- **Expected outcome:** the closest big workforce (Humber Health about 20k staff [S27]) builds a habit; nominations spread through hospital WhatsApp groups. *Check the kitchen hours: Mon–Thu kitchen closes at 20:00, which misses many shift ends. Consider Raph's pizza to 21:00.*

**12. "Mystery Dish Thursday" (Cassiel)**
- **Format:** Reel, then a Story poll.
- **Hook:** *"Guess what's in this and it's yours, free."*
- **Script:** a close-up of the chef's special → poll or comments → reveal Friday → available Thursdays only, in limited numbers.
- **Cadence:** fortnightly.
- **Expected outcome:** comments (engagement); a reason to visit on Thursday; lets the kitchen test new menu items.

**13. "Dinner Here, Party There": the Hallgate Crawl reel (joint Collab post)**
- **Format:** one continuous walk-and-talk shot: Cassiel table → out of the door → 30 metres → into Raph's as the band starts.
- **Hook:** *"The best Christmas party in Cottingham has two venues."*
- **Cadence:** a hero piece for W2, with Meta budget behind it; re-cut for NYE.
- **Expected outcome:** explains the USP in 20 seconds; the main Christmas lead-driver.

**14. "POV: You're Organising the Work Do" (both)**
- **Format:** a comedy Reel acted by staff.
- **Hook:** *"POV: you've been put in charge of the work Christmas do."*
- **Script:** panicked organiser → spreadsheets → "Then I found a place that does dinner AND a band next door AND the organiser eats free" → "Send this to whoever's organising yours."
- **Cadence:** 2–3 variants in W2–W6.
- **Expected outcome:** **sends** to colleagues (the top non-follower signal [S9]); party enquiries.

**15. Local creator collabs (both)**
- **Format:** creator Reels and TikToks posted as Instagram Collab posts.
- **Hook:** set by the creator (their style), with the brief "honest first impressions".
- **Plan:** shortlist 6–10 Hull and East Riding food or lifestyle micro-creators (2k–30k local followers) from Instagram hashtags such as #hullfood and #hulleats, and from creator directories such as Collabstr (no verified Hull food-creator list was found [S28]). Invite 3–5 to the **Creator Evening (Wed 18 Nov)** and 3–5 to **Round Two (Wed 13 Jan)**. Arrangement: gifted meal and drinks, no script, disclosed as #gifted or #ad.
- **Expected outcome:** 5–10 posts reaching 20–60k local views; social proof for Raph's food. **Only invite them once the kitchen is consistent**: one bad creator night can't be undone.

**16. UGC competition: "Hallgate Christmas Jumper Fridays" (both)**
- **Format:** guests post a photo in a festive jumper at either venue and tag the venue. Weekly winner: a £50 gift card.
- **Hook:** *"Wear the jumper, win the tab."*
- **Cadence:** Fridays from 27 Nov to 18 Dec.
- **Expected outcome:** a steady flow of guest content to repost (with permission); a reason to dress up; low cost (£200 face value over 4 weeks, well below that at cost).

**17. "The Gift That Isn't Socks" (both)**
- **Format:** a Reel unboxing a gift card plus an experience card.
- **Hook:** *"Stop buying him socks."*
- **Script:** sad socks → a Hallgate gift card → "Tapas and cocktails for two, £49. Delivered by email in 2 minutes."
- **Cadence:** W5–W11, with spikes on Black Friday and Mad Friday.
- **Expected outcome:** voucher sales; sends to partners.

**18. "Plate vs Price": transparency (Raph's, then Cassiel)**
- **Format:** a top-down Reel with prices and components labelled on screen.
- **Hook:** *"This is what £21 gets you now."* (the rebuilt gyros or a hero dish)
- **Script:** each component named and where it comes from (the local butcher, named only with their agreement; dough made in-house) → "Judge for yourself."
- **Cadence:** W6, then monthly.
- **Expected outcome:** directly answers value-for-money doubts; **only claim what is true** (frozen fries are fine to use, but don't imply everything is fresh).

**19. "Vinyl Sunday" or "Bring Your Own Record" (Raph's, if trading Sundays)**
- **Format:** an FB Event, then Reels of guests' records being played.
- **Hook:** *"Bring a record. We'll play one track from it. Tell us why it matters."*
- **Cadence:** monthly, first Sunday (or Thursday evening).
- **Expected outcome:** cheap event, memorable stories, very shareable clips among the 40–65 audience.

**20. "Taste It First": tasting-night recruitment (Raph's)**
- **Format:** an FB post in local groups, plus the comment-to-enter mechanic from idea #1.
- **Hook:** *"Wanted: 60 brutally honest Cottingham locals."*
- **Script:** "Free tasting menu. All we ask is a written verdict, good or bad."
- **Cadence:** W3–W4.
- **Expected outcome:** 60 diners and feedback cards; word of mouth. **No reviews are asked for in return** (section 6.4).

**21. "Pizza for a Cause" community fundraiser (Raph's) and "Tapas for a Cause" (Cassiel)**
- **Format:** FB, IG and a local press release.
- **Hook:** *"Every pizza on Tuesday = £1 to [local charity]."* (e.g. a Hull hospice or a hospital charity; confirm the partner and get their permission before naming them.)
- **Cadence:** monthly, with a different local cause each month.
- **Expected outcome:** the charity shares to its own followers; community goodwill; a midweek lift. Cost: £1 per item, about 7% of a £14 bundle.

**22. "Hallgate Insider": the WhatsApp Channel (both)**
- **Format:** a WhatsApp Channel (free, follow-based, one-to-many). Weekly: "This week on Hallgate" (5 lines); Friday "last tables"; first access to NYE and Valentine's tickets; Channel-only codes ("say INSIDER for a free bread & olives").
- **Hook:** *"First dibs. Last-minute tables. No spam."*
- **Cadence:** 2–3 posts a week.
- **Expected outcome:** 450 followers by mid-January; a direct channel that doesn't depend on algorithms; measurable through codes.

**23. "Organiser Pack": a lead magnet (both; email and Meta lead form)**
- **Format:** a 4-page PDF (menus, tiers, timings, FAQ, deposit terms, the Crawl explained, an allergen promise), offered in Reels as "Comment PACK and we'll DM it" or through a Meta instant form.
- **Hook:** *"Organising the Christmas do? Steal our planner."*
- **Cadence:** always on from W2 to W8.
- **Expected outcome:** a qualified organiser list; enquiry-to-booking ≥40%.

**24. "January Joy List" and "Zero-Proof Club" (Cassiel); "Pizza Reboot" (Raph's)**
- **Format:** a carousel of "5 joyful things to do on Hallgate this January", Reels of 0% cocktail builds, and a TikTok "rating our 0% cocktails out of 10" with real customers.
- **Hook:** *"Doing Dry January? You still deserve a proper cocktail."*
- **Cadence:** W12–W15.
- **Expected outcome:** captures the roughly 17.5m-strong dry or damp audience [S6]; midweek visits.

**Bonus formats:**
- **"Then vs Now"** split screen (old pizza vs new pizza, Raph's, W5).
- **"Rate my order"**: guests rate their plate on camera (with permission).
- **Google Business Profile "This week on Hallgate"** posts every Monday (section 8.1).
- **"Ask the Owners"** monthly Stories Q&A, to keep the owners visible.
- **"Student Night"** only if verified demand exists (see section 1; a low priority).
