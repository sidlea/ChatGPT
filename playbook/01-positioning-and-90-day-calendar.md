# Positioning and the 90-day comeback calendar (13 Oct 2026 to 24 Jan 2027)

> Part of the Hallgate playbook for Cassiel Bar & Kitchen and Raph's Lounge, Cottingham. Prices, dates and margins are working assumptions (Oct 2026): check them against real recipe costs, licences and calendars before use.

## 2. Strategic frame: positioning, de-cannibalisation and margin rules

### 2.1 Give each venue one job
| | **Cassiel Bar & Kitchen** | **Raph's Lounge** |
|---|---|---|
| One-line role | *"Cottingham's cocktail bar and Mediterranean kitchen"*: small plates, fish, grills, proper cocktails | *"The Lounge in the old bank"*: live music, pizza and grill, cold drinks, parties, sport |
| Core occasions | Weekday lunch tapas, date night, friends' catch-up, cocktail masterclass, sit-down Christmas parties of 6–30 | Fri/Sat nights out, live bands, quiz, birthdays and private hire, standing Christmas parties, Boxing Day and NYE |
| Hero products | Tapas (Tapa Nights), meat and mezze boards, fish, cocktails, zero-proof cocktails | Pizza (new dough, real mozzarella), steak night, sharing platters, pints, live music |
| Owns the cuisine | **Mediterranean, including Greek** (gyros or souvlaki as tapas) | **Pizza and grill** (a burger can stay, but one great one, not three average ones) |
| Tone | Warm, a little elegant, "temperance and taste" | Loud, local, sociable, "cold drinks, good company" |

**Brand thread:** both venues are named after archangels (Cassiel, and Raph(ael)), under the Archangel Group. Use it lightly in seasonal creative, for example a Halloween "Angels and Demons" crawl, without making it the brand.

### 2.3 Margin-aware offer rules (put these in the Content Brain as "Offer Guardrails")
1. **Add value; don't cut the price.** Give low-cost, high-perceived-value items (dough balls, bread and olives, prosecco, a mini dessert) instead of % off. Target giveaway cost: **≤8% of the transaction**.
2. **Discount only dead time.** Mon–Thu, 12–6pm, and January. **No discounts on Fri/Sat in December.**
3. **Bundle high-GP items.** Pizza, tapas and cocktails carry the most margin; steaks and fish carry less.
4. **Everything has a tracking key:** a code word, a QR, a booking note, or "Say 'Hallgate' at the bar".
5. **Time-box every offer** with an end date that is shown on the post.
6. **Never run the same offer in both venues on the same night.**
7. **Deposits on every group of 8+:** £10pp, pre-order 14 days ahead, balance 7 days ahead. This reduces no-shows and brings cash forward.
8. **Fix allergen handling before scaling groups.** Every party pre-order form asks about the 14 allergens. Weak allergy handling already appears in Cassiel's reviews.

### 3.3 Hero campaign specifications

#### Hero 1: "Christmas on Hallgate" (both venues; 13 Oct to 24 Dec)
- **Concept:** "Two venues. One street. One great Christmas do." Small groups book the venue that fits them; bigger groups book the **Hallgate Christmas Crawl**: dinner at Cassiel, then 30 metres across the road to Raph's for the band. No coach, no taxi, no splitting up.
- **Mechanics:** three tiers per venue plus the Crawl (prices in section 5.2). £10pp deposit; pre-order and allergen form 14 days ahead; balance 7 days ahead. Deadlines: **early-bird by 31 Oct** (free prosecco per 8 guests, costing about £0.75 a head); **book by 15 Nov** (the organiser eats free for groups of 12+, worth about 8% of the bill). Lunch parties Mon–Thu at 1pm at a lower price point.
- **Target segments:** (1) work groups of 8–40 within 10 miles (Castle Hill and HUTH teams, schools, Hallgate offices, trades); (2) friends' groups of 6–12 (mostly women aged 30–60, the strongest Facebook audience); (3) families wanting a festive meal out.
- **Channels:** Google Business Profile Products and Offer posts; Google Search (section 8); Meta (an organiser Reel boosted to a 10-mile radius); Facebook local groups (owner posting as himself, within group rules); email and WhatsApp to the list; A-boards and window posters; a downloadable **"Organiser Pack" PDF** (menus, tiers, timings, FAQ, deposit terms) that is emailed on enquiry.
- **Budget:** about £600 of the paid total (Google about £400, Meta about £200).
- **KPIs:** party covers booked against capacity targets (25% by 31 Oct, 50% by 15 Nov, 80% by 6 Dec, 95% of Fri/Sat slots by 13 Dec); enquiry-to-booking conversion ≥40%; deposit cash collected; average spend per head ≥ package price + £12 in drinks.

#### Hero 2: "Raph's Kitchen Reset" → "Round Two" (Raph's; 13 Oct to 17 Jan)
The full playbook is in section 6. Budget about £250 (Meta behind V2 and V3, creator food cost at roughly 30% of menu price). KPIs: 25+ new Google reviews averaging ≥4.5 by 17 Jan; zero new reviews mentioning "frozen" or "fake cheese"; food sales mix at Raph's up 20% on September.

#### Hero 3: Hallgate Gift Cards (both; on sale 9 Nov to 24 Dec, then all year)
- **Products:** £25, £50 and £100 cash cards, plus experiences: *Tapas & Cocktails for Two* £49 (Cassiel), *Cocktail Masterclass for Two* £55 (Cassiel), *Steak Night for Two* £45 (Raph's, after the relaunch), *Gig Night for Two* (pizza, 2 pints, band) £35 (Raph's).
- **Black Friday mechanic (27–30 Nov):** buy £50 or more and get a £10 Bonus Card valid **11 Jan to 31 Mar 2027**, Mon–Thu. That moves a redemption visit into the dead season, and the guest usually spends above the card value.
- **Channels:** Wix gift card or e-voucher (instant email), a counter display with physical cards, an email to the list, "The gift that isn't socks" Reel, Google Business Profile Offer post, and the Christmas Festival stand.
- **Watch-outs:** The two venues are run by separate companies, so either sell venue-specific cards, or agree a written inter-company settlement and a VAT treatment (multi-purpose voucher, so VAT is due on redemption) with the accountant **before** selling a joint "Hallgate" card. Give cash cards at least 12 months' validity and state the expiry clearly.
- **KPI:** £6,000 face value by 24 Dec (stretch £10k); 30% of buyers opt into the list.

#### Hero 4: NYE on Hallgate (both; on sale from 16 Nov)
- **Cassiel NYE Supper:** 6:00 sitting £39.95 (5 festive tapas + a cocktail, finishing at 8:15); 8:30 sitting £54.95 (5 tapas, dessert, a glass of fizz, and the table until close).
- **Raph's "NYE at the Old Bank":** £15 ticket (arrival drink, band and DJ). Late hours depend on the licence: **check the NYE permitted hours now**; if a Temporary Event Notice is needed, submit it in good time (a standard TEN needs at least 10 working days' notice, so aim for 1 Dec).
- **Hallgate NYE Pass £64.95:** the Cassiel 8:30 sitting plus Raph's entry for midnight.
- **KPI:** sold out by 24 Dec; 100% prepaid.

#### Hero 5: "January Joy on Hallgate" (both; 2–31 Jan)
- **Concept:** "Joy, not guilt." January as a treat on a budget, with no lecturing about health.
- **Mechanics:** Zero-Proof Club (Cassiel: 0% cocktails £5.50, or 3 tapas + a 0% cocktail £21.90 Mon–Thu); Plant Tapas 3 for £14.90; Raph's Pizza & Pint £14 (Tue), Steak Night £19.95 (Wed), Quiz (Thu); **double loyalty stamps Mon–Thu**; Bonus Cards redeemable from 11 Jan; Blue Monday £5 blue cocktail or mocktail.
- **Target:** locals, NHS staff, Damp January drinkers, and Christmas guests (win-back email 10 days after their visit).
- **KPIs:** Mon–Thu covers +15% vs January 2026 (if records exist), or vs November midweek; 0% drinks ≥10% of drinks transactions at Cassiel; 300 loyalty sign-ups.
