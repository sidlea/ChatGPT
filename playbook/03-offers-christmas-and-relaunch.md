### 5.3 Loyalty: the recommended set-up
- **Weeks 1–12: a paper "Hallgate Passport" plus list sign-up.** It costs nothing, works for the Christmas Festival and Halloween, and keeps the December team focused on service.
- **W13 (Jan): launch the digital "Hallgate Club"** using one QR stamp-card platform for both venues: Stamp Me, Loyalzoo or Squid, at about £40–60 a month [S19]. Choose by: (a) a free trial; (b) the ability to have two locations in one programme; (c) exporting or syncing contacts to the Google Sheet or Wix (CSV at minimum, ideally an API or Zapier/n8n connection); (d) a GDPR consent field.
- **Reward ladder:**
  - Sign-up: a 0% cocktail or bread & olives.
  - 3 stamps: a free dessert or dough balls.
  - 6 stamps: £10 to spend.
  - Birthday-month reward.
  - Double stamps Mon–Thu in January.
- **Ask whether SV Tables offers loyalty or marketing:** "SV Tables" appears to be **Sugarvine Tables** (unverified, from Trustpilot reviews [S29]), and its features weren't confirmed. If it has a guest-marketing module, use that first to avoid paying for another tool.

### 5.4 Takeaway and delivery: pros, cons and recommendation (Raph's pizza only)
| | Marketplaces (Just Eat / Uber Eats / Deliveroo) | Direct (own website ordering, collection) |

## 6. Raph's food relaunch playbook ("Kitchen Reset" → "Round Two")

### 6.1 The principle
**Fix, then prove privately, then prove publicly, then amplify.** A relaunch is only credible if (a) the product has *visibly* changed, (b) people outside the business say so, and (c) the owners put their own faces on it. Never promote the food before the kitchen has served the new menu consistently for about 10 trading days. A creator visit or a viral post on a bad night can't be undone.

### 6.2 Product fixes that make the story true (agree these with the menu and ops workstream)
| Area | Fix | Proof shot for content |
|---|---|---|
| Pizza cheese | Real mozzarella (whole-milk low-moisture for the base, optionally fior di latte as a premium). Menu wording must describe exactly what is used: calling a substitute "mozzarella" is a misdescription risk | A side-by-side stretch test |
| Dough | In-house dough with a 24–48h cold ferment (ingredients cost pennies per ball; needs a mixer and a proving routine) | Dough timelapse, then the blistered crust |
| Chicken | Marinated chicken thigh, char-grilled to order | The grill-mark close-up |
| Tzatziki | Grated, salted, squeezed cucumber; thick yoghurt; garlic; dill or mint; made daily. **Or move Greek dishes to Cassiel** (section 2.1) | A spoon-stand test |
| Gyros plate value | Remove it, or rebuild the value (generous portion, proper flatbread, fries, salad, dips) at a lower price point. Better still, sell it at Cassiel as tapas | "Plate vs Price" Reel (idea #18) |
| Bought-in components | These can stay if they're good, but **never claim "everything fresh"** (a misleading-claim risk). Talk only about what you actually make | — |
| A long menu across too many cuisines | 12–15 items: pizza (6), grill (3: steak, chicken, one burger), sharers (3), desserts (2) | "The new menu fits on one page" |

**Go/no-go gate:** if the tasting-night average is **below 8/10**, or the same criticism appears on more than 25% of cards, delay the public reveal (13 Nov) by one week and fix it first.

### 6.4 Review rules (protect the venue's reputation and the business)
- **Ask every guest, not just the happy ones.** Send a post-visit email or SMS, or hand over a bill card with a QR code to Google reviews. Selectively soliciting only positive reviews ("review gating") breaches Google's policy (not re-verified in this pass).
- **Never offer anything in exchange for a review**, and never post reviews written by staff or family. Google's policies prohibit incentivised and fake reviews, and the UK **Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024** banned fake reviews and concealed incentivised reviews from April 2025 (not re-verified in this pass; check the CMA guidance).
- **Tasting-night guests get the free meal in exchange for *private written feedback***, not reviews. If they choose to post publicly, ask them to mention that they were invited.
- Keep the existing **review-response approval system**, and add a rule: any reply about food quality is signed by Simon by name and names a specific change.

### 6.5 Messaging guardrails
- Say "new kitchen", "made here", "real mozzarella", "local butcher". Don't say "all fresh" or "nothing frozen" unless it is literally true.
- Don't mention internal matters (legal, property or finance) in any public content. Supplier names come from internal data, so only name a supplier publicly with their agreement.
- If the owners would rather not address the reviews head-on, the **quiet version** of V1 is: *"New season at Raph's: we've rebuilt our kitchen from the dough up. Taste it first."* (The same tasting nights, without mentioning the criticism. Less powerful, but lower risk.)
